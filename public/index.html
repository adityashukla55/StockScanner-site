The whole loop, one tab

Add a ticker, check what it is, see how it compares, enter what you hold, project it forward, then weigh the mix — and go round again when something changes. Five views over one watchlist, sorted into groups if you like, none of which sends you to another site.

Watchlist and detail

Live quotes every 15 seconds, paused while the tab is hidden. Candles from two minutes to twenty years, with company fundamentals alongside.

Groups

Sort tickers into groups — India, Europe, EUR, Tech, whatever you follow. Pick one and every tab shows only that group, from the watchlist to the portfolio totals. Fill a group in one click by country or currency.

Compare

Up to eight tickers rebased to a common start, so relative performance is readable rather than inferred from two charts side by side — across exchanges and currencies alike.

Portfolio

Enter what your broker shows: the amount invested, the fee and the price per share. Shares, value and gain are worked out for you — fractional shares too. Type any price to see what your shares would be worth. Each currency is totalled on its own.

Projection

A lognormal model fitted to each ticker, drawn as percentile bands. Where options trade, volatility comes from the option market rather than from the past.

Pick your market

Follow one market — India, Germany, Japan, Canada, wherever you actually invest — and search narrows to it while the clock reads its local hours. Switch any time, or choose All markets. It filters what you discover, never what you already hold.

Allocation

Split a sum across your picks and see what it could become, as a total and as a yearly rate. Fit buttons show what the maths suggests and are careful to say what they are not.

Backup & move

New computer? Download your setup as a file or copy it as text, then load it there — add to what you have or replace it, with undo. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Private by construction, not by promise

Most tools ask you to trust a privacy policy. This one has no server to send anything to, which is a different sort of claim — one you can check by reading the source.

No accountNothing to sign up for.
No serverPrices go straight from Yahoo to your browser.
No telemetryNo analytics, no error reporting, no pings.
Holdings stay yoursWhat you invested and hold lives in browser storage alone.
Backups stay with youA backup is a file you save yourself. It is never uploaded.

It does not request tabs, reads no page you visit, and injects no content script. It talks to no host but Yahoo.

It shows. It never touches money.

Stock Scanner is a viewer. It reads public market data and draws it. There is no path through this extension by which a single currency unit could move, because none of the machinery to do so exists in it.

✕No brokerage connectionIt links to no broker, bank or exchange account. There is nothing to connect.
✕No tradingNo buy or sell button exists. It cannot place, cancel or route an order.
✕No paymentsIt never asks for card, bank or wallet details, and has no payment code in it.
✕No credentialsNo logins to anything. The only permission beyond the price feed is browser storage.

The holdings you type into the Portfolio tab are notes to yourself — numbers you enter so the app can multiply them by a price. They are not a position, not linked to an account, and visible to nobody but you.

What it does not do

Stock Scanner is for information only. It makes no promise about the future, and nothing in it should be read as one.

It describes the pastEvery number starts from prices that have already happened.
It draws possibilitiesProjections are mathematical functions over that history, not predictions.
It recommends nothingNo buy or sell signals, no portfolio advice, no opinion on any ticker.
It is not adviceNot investment, financial, tax or legal advice. What you do with the numbers is yours.
